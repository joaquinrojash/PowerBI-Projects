## 📁 Project Summaries

### 1. 👩‍💼 Data Science Job Market  
An analysis of job market trends for data science roles using job listings data. The dashboard helps identify in-demand skills, location hotspots, and salary trends.  
> **Objective:** Empower job seekers and recruiters with a clear picture of the data science employment landscape.  
> **Skills Used:** Custom visuals, slicers, DAX for calculations, Power Query for cleaning data.

### 2. 🧑‍💻 Employee Presenteeism  
An analytics dashboard exploring the impact of presenteeism (working while sick) across a company’s workforce using attendance data.  
> **Objective:** Reveal behavioral patterns, lost productivity, and HR intervention opportunities.  
> **Skills Used:** Excel integration, date/time logic, calculated columns, filters for demographic insights.

### 3. 🧾 HR Analytics  
An all-in-one HR dashboard exploring employee retention, demographics, and performance metrics to support strategic HR decision-making.  
> **Objective:** Give HR managers a holistic view of employee dynamics and workforce planning indicators.  
> **Skills Used:** Data modeling, KPI visualizations, DAX metrics for tenure and attrition, interactive reports.
